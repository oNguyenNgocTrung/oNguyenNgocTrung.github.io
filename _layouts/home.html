--- layout: page --- {{ content }} {% assign posts = paginator.posts | default: site.posts %} {%- comment -%} DIVERGES FROM UPSTREAM. Two changes, both deliberate: 1. Posts render differently by kind. Articles keep upstream's full preview (thumbnail, excerpt, Read More). TILs and Journals collapse to a single line, because they are short by definition and a 50-word excerpt of a three-line post is just the post again. The reason this matters: TILs get written far more often than Articles. With one uniform rendering the home stream silently becomes a wall of one-liners and the long pieces drown. Sizing the preview to the kind keeps the page honest about what is on it - everything shows up, but the eye still lands on the Articles. 2. Tag links point at /topics, not /tags. "Tag" is Jekyll's mechanism; "topic" is the word this site uses with its readers. {%- endcomment -%}
